FAQ and troubleshooting

Requirements, codec support and fixes for common problems.

Requirements

What do I need to run AdamApp?

Windows 10 (1809+) or 11, 64-bit, 8 GB RAM recommended, and a DirectX 11 GPU.

Does it work offline?

Yes. AdamApp makes no network requests while you edit and sends no telemetry.

Is it free?

Yes. It is open source with no watermark or feature limits.

Codecs

Which formats can I import?

MP4, MOV and MKV with H.264 or H.265 video, WAV audio and PNG stills.

A file will not import.

Run adamapp probe file.mkv to see its codec. Re-encode unsupported codecs to H.264 first.

Troubleshooting

SmartScreen blocks the .exe.

Choose More info, then Run anyway. Verify the SHA-256 checksum first.

Exports are slow.

Check that hardware encoding is active, or force it with --hw nvenc, qsv or amf. Update your GPU driver.

Playback stutters.

Close other GPU-heavy apps and lower preview resolution in the preview menu.
