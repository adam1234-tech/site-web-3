Features and workflows

The timeline, audio tools, effects and shortcuts.

Timeline

The timeline has unlimited video and audio tracks. Cuts snap to frame boundaries. Scrub by dragging the playhead; the preview is GPU-composited and cached in RAM.

  • Split, trim, ripple delete and move clips.
  • Lock, mute or solo any track.
  • Zoom to a single frame or to the whole project.

Audio

Each track has gain, pan and a loudness meter. WAV files import with sample-accurate sync. Use the Audio tab to match a target loudness before export.

Effects and color

The Color tab covers lift, gamma, gain and saturation. Effects are applied per clip and rendered on the GPU, so adjusting them does not interrupt playback.
