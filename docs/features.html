Timeline, tools and shortcuts

Reference for editing, audio, color and export.

Timeline tracks

Add any number of video and audio tracks. Lock, mute or solo each track. Cuts snap to frame boundaries and zoom goes down to a single frame.

Trimming and splitting

  • Split at the playhead with S.
  • Trim by dragging a clip edge.
  • Ripple delete closes the gap on all unlocked tracks.

Audio and video controls

Each audio track has gain, pan and a level meter. Video clips have transform, opacity and a lift, gamma and gain color panel, all rendered on the GPU.
