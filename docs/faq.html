FAQ and troubleshooting

Privacy, codecs, hardware acceleration and Windows fixes.

Privacy

Does LocalCut upload my media?

No. There are no cloud features, accounts or telemetry. Projects and exports stay on disk.

Is it free?

Yes, with no watermark or feature limits.

Containers and codecs

What can I import and export?

MP4, MKV and MOV containers with H.264 or H.265 video, WAV audio, and PNG stills.

A file will not import.

Run localcut probe file.mkv to see its codec, and re-encode unsupported codecs to H.264.

Hardware acceleration

Is my GPU used for encoding?

LocalCut uses NVENC, Quick Sync or AMF when available. Force one with --hw nvenc, qsv or amf, or use --hw cpu to disable it.

Exports are slow.

Update your GPU driver and confirm hardware encoding is active.

Windows issues

SmartScreen blocks localcut.exe.

Choose More info, then Run anyway, after verifying the SHA-256 hash.

The app will not start.

Confirm Windows is 64-bit and extract the full zip. Delete %APPDATA%\LocalCut\settings.json to reset settings.

Playback stutters.

Close other GPU-heavy apps and lower the preview resolution.
